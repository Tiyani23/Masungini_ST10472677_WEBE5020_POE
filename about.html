Who We Are

Creating hope and opportunities for communities across Gauteng.

OUR STORY

Hope Through Action

Hope Hands Community Foundation is a non-profit organisation established in 2019 to support disadvantaged families, young people and vulnerable communities across Gauteng.

Our work focuses on education, food relief, youth development and community outreach. We work with volunteers, donors, corporate sponsors and community partners to create sustainable positive change.

We believe that small acts of kindness can create meaningful and lasting change.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable communities by providing education, food assistance and opportunities that improve quality of life.

Our Vision

To create stronger communities where every individual has access to education, support and equal opportunities.
